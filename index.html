Overview & Focus

Bridging Fundamental Machine Learning with Real-World Impact

Pioneering algorithms in computer vision, deep neural networks, and generative modeling across academia and world-leading industrial research.

I am a Senior Machine Learning Scientist & Tech Lead at L'Oréal Research & Innovation in Paris, where I lead high-impact research projects focusing on deep learning and computer vision for 3D human face and hair modeling, neural rendering, and real-time virtual try-on systems.

Concurrently, I serve as a Part-time Adjunct Professor at NEOMA Business School in Paris within the Master of Finance & Big Data program, teaching comprehensive machine learning curricula covering supervised learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, and modern Generative AI.

With an academic foundation from EPFL (PhD, IDIAP Research Institute) and ENS Cachan (Master MVA), my career spans key research positions at INRIA, Dailymotion, VideoStitch, and Télécom Bretagne.

Generative AI & Inverse Graphics

Differentiable rendering, neural 3D modeling for human face and hair, virtual try-on, and real-time synthesis.

Computer Vision & Video Analysis

Multi-person tracking, head-pose estimation, gaze direction, and 360-degree panoramic video processing.

Multimodal Audio-Visual AI

Joint audio-visual observation models, active speaker detection, speaker diarization, and human-robot interaction.

Variational Bayesian & Inverse Problems

Variational data assimilation, Rao-Blackwellized particle filtering, Gaussian mixtures, and missing data interpolation.
